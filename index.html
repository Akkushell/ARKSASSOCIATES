About Us

Environmental consulting expertise for MPCB and CGWA regulatory compliance.

ARK'S ASSOCIATES is a dedicated environmental consulting firm specializing in water treatment systems, groundwater management, and pollution control solutions for Maharashtra-based industries and municipalities. With a deep understanding of MPCB and CGWA regulatory frameworks, we provide end-to-end support from project conception through operational excellence.

Our team combines technical engineering expertise with regulatory knowledge to deliver cost-effective, compliant environmental solutions. We work with industrial clients, municipal corporations, and government agencies to design, permit, and operate STP, ETP, ZLD systems, and comprehensive groundwater management programs.

  • Regulatory Expertise: Specialized knowledge of MPCB Consent to Establish/Operate and CGWA NOC requirements
  • Integrated Solutions: STP, ETP, ZLD, RO, filtration, and air pollution control in one partnership
  • Site-Specific Design: Customized environmental systems tailored to project requirements and regulatory limits
  • Ongoing O&M Support: Operational maintenance, troubleshooting, and compliance monitoring services
  • Fast-Track Approvals: Streamlined application preparation and regulatory liaison for quick permitting
  • Quality Assurance: Audit-ready documentation and performance monitoring throughout project lifecycle
