Our Story

ARK'S ASSOCIATES is a group of Engineers & Technocrats dedicated to environmental compliance, water treatment, and regulatory solutions across India. Founded on the principle of engineering excellence, our team combines deep technical expertise with practical regulatory knowledge to deliver sustainable solutions.

Under the leadership of Mr. Abhinay Gajbhiye and Mr. Rahul Walondre, we've successfully guided more than 400 projects to regulatory approval and operational excellence. We believe in the importance of sustainability and aim to reduce the impact on the environment to the minimum.

Our team understands the complexity of India's environmental regulations and the unique challenges each project faces. We work with clients to navigate regulatory frameworks, reduce approval timelines, and ensure systems operate efficiently and compliantly while maintaining our core commitment to environmental stewardship and sustainable practices.

Our Work

Comprehensive environmental compliance solutions

We handle every aspect of environmental regulatory requirements and water treatment system delivery.

🏛️

CPCB/SPCB Consent Clearance

End-to-end support for Central and State Pollution Control Board approvals, including detailed project reports, environmental compliance plans, and regulatory submissions across all states.

  • Consent to Establish (CTE) applications
  • Consent to Operate (CTO) renewals
  • Environmental clearance support
  • Compliance documentation
💧

CGWA Groundwater NOC

Expert guidance for Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) no-objection certificates, including groundwater assessment, aquifer analysis, and sustainable extraction planning.

  • Groundwater feasibility studies
  • CGWA NOC applications
  • Aquifer mapping and analysis
  • Sustainable water management
🏭

STP & ETP Engineering

Complete design, engineering, and commissioning of Sewage Treatment Plants and Effluent Treatment Plants tailored to industry and discharge requirements.

  • Process selection and optimization
  • Civil and mechanical design
  • System commissioning and startup
  • Performance validation testing

  • Waste management solutions
