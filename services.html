Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

End-to-end STP solutions for residential, commercial and industrial applications. We cover process selection, civil layout, biological treatment design, commissioning support, compliance with MPCB norms and post-installation performance monitoring.

✓

Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)

Specialized ETP design and implementation for industrial wastewater streams. Our service includes process selection, equalization, biological and chemical treatment, sludge handling, discharge quality assurance, and MPCB consent support.

✓

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

Comprehensive ZLD engineering to minimize waste and recover water from industrial effluents. We design concentration, evaporation, thermal and mechanical recovery systems, plus a complete recycle strategy to meet zero discharge goals.

✓

Operation & Maintenance

Reliable O&M support for STP, ETP, ZLD, RO and filtration systems. Our team provides planned maintenance, troubleshooting, process optimization, operator training and regular performance audits to maximize uptime and regulatory compliance.

✓

Ground Water (CGWA) NOC

CGWA groundwater permission services including hydrogeological survey, borewell assessment, drilling plan review, yield analysis and application management for safe abstraction and regulatory approval.

✓

MPCB Consent Clearance

Full MPCB consent services for Consent to Establish, Consent to Operate and renewals. We prepare compliance documentation, monitoring plans, supporting reports and liaison with authorities to improve approval timelines.

✓

Environmental Clearance

Environmental clearance support with EIA study coordination, statutory submissions, compliance verification and public hearing assistance. We guide projects through state and central clearance requirements for sustainable development.

✓

Water Treatment Systems

Technical consulting for RO, softener, filtration, dosing and packaged water treatment systems. Our team helps select equipment, optimize process flow, manage procurement and ensure treated water quality for reuse or potable use.
